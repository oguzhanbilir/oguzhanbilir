# EduPredict

Students preparing for the university entrance exam wanted to know how close their mock exam results were to the programme they were aiming for.

## What I built

A dashboard where students enter mock exam scores and compare them with past years' cut-off values, per subject.

## Key features

- Shows the gap per subject and a placement estimate
- Student, counsellor and admin roles
- Counsellors bulk-upload their students' mock results; admins import cut-off data
- A credit system with a demo payment screen, prototype only and not connected to any real payment or ad service

## Stack

Python, FastAPI, SQLite, a single-page HTML front end

## My role

I designed and built the backend and interface.

## Outcome

A working prototype. The sample cut-off data is for demonstration.
